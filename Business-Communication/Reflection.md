#  Reflection — Business Communication (STAR)

**Situation:**  
During my Work Readiness training, I participated in a professional communication workshop where we had to deliver a formal business presentation and compose an email to a simulated client. The activity focused on clarity, tone, and structure in a business context.

**Task:**  
My objective was to demonstrate effective written and verbal communication skills by preparing a concise, audience-appropriate presentation and drafting a professional email that conveyed information clearly and respectfully.

**Action:**  
To prepare, I researched effective business writing principles such as clarity, brevity, and tone management.  
I applied these principles in my email by using a formal greeting, clear subject line, and a structured message body.  
For my presentation, I practiced using confident body language, maintaining eye contact, and pacing my delivery to ensure my message was understood.  
I also sought feedback from my peers and lecturer to refine both my written and spoken communication.

**Result:**  
I successfully delivered my presentation with confidence and received positive feedback on my ability to engage the audience and communicate ideas clearly. My written communication improved significantly—I learned to avoid informal expressions and maintain a professional tone.  
This exercise helped me understand how good communication strengthens teamwork, builds professional credibility, and minimizes misunderstandings in a workplace environment.

**Skills Developed:**  
- Business email etiquette  
- Verbal presentation and confidence  
- Listening and feedback incorporation  
- Structuring and tailoring communication for different audiences

**Future Improvement:**  
I plan to further improve by developing concise reporting skills and using tools like Grammarly and business templates to refine my tone and accuracy in professional communication.
