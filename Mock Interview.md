# Reflection — Mock Interview (STAR)

**Situation:**  
As part of the Work Readiness programme, I participated in a mock interview designed to simulate a real-world job interview for an entry-level IT position. The interviewer assessed my communication style, professionalism, and ability to answer behavioral questions.

**Task:**  
My objective was to apply the communication and interview principles learned in class — particularly the **STAR technique** — to demonstrate my readiness for professional interviews. I needed to convey my skills clearly and make a strong first impression.

**Action:**  
To prepare, I reviewed common technical and behavioral interview questions and practiced responses with peers.  
During the mock interview, I focused on maintaining good posture, clear articulation, and a positive tone.  
When asked questions like “Tell me about a time you solved a problem,” I structured my answers using the STAR method. I also asked insightful questions at the end to show engagement and curiosity.

**Result:**  
The feedback highlighted my confidence, strong verbal delivery, and structured responses.  
However, I was advised to improve my pacing — to pause briefly before answering and avoid rushing responses.  
The experience gave me real insight into professional interview dynamics and how preparation affects confidence and flow.

**Skills Developed:**  
- STAR-based storytelling  
- Professional body language and eye contact  
- Time management during interviews  
- Self-assessment and feedback interpretation  

**Future Improvement:**  
I plan to record more mock sessions to refine my timing and enhance my ability to stay composed under pressure.  
My next goal is to strengthen my technical interview responses by aligning them more closely with job-specific requirements.
