#  Reflection — Workplace Etiquette (STAR)

**Situation:**  
During the Work Readiness programme, I participated in a training session focused on workplace etiquette — covering topics such as communication styles, time management, and workplace respect.  
The aim was to help students understand how to behave professionally in an organizational environment.

**Task:**  
My objective was to demonstrate professionalism through proper etiquette, both in face-to-face and online communication.  
I needed to apply these principles in simulated workplace tasks, such as attending virtual meetings, responding to emails, and presenting myself appropriately.

**Action:**  
I practiced punctuality and professionalism during class sessions and group activities.  
I made sure to communicate respectfully in group projects, using clear and polite language when giving feedback.  
I also researched company codes of conduct and applied these principles to my interactions.  
In addition, I created a short presentation summarizing the importance of workplace etiquette and shared it with peers for feedback.

**Result:**  
The experience helped me understand how professional behavior directly affects workplace relationships and reputation.  
Feedback indicated that my communication had improved and that I demonstrated good teamwork and reliability.  
I also realized how small habits — like being on time, maintaining a respectful tone, and following through on commitments — build trust in a professional environment.

**Skills Developed:**  
- Professional communication and tone management  
- Punctuality and accountability  
- Respectful collaboration and teamwork  
- Adaptability in different work environments  

**Future Improvement:**  
I plan to continue improving by observing professional workplace norms and developing emotional intelligence to manage stress and maintain positive interactions under pressure.
