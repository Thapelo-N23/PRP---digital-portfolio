#  Reflection — Professional Networking (STAR)

**Situation:**  
During the Work Readiness programme, I was tasked with developing my professional network using platforms such as LinkedIn.  
I needed to demonstrate how networking can enhance employability and professional growth in the ICT field.

**Task:**  
My objective was to build a credible professional profile, connect with relevant individuals in the tech industry, and engage in meaningful professional interactions.  
I aimed to reflect an image of competence, reliability, and initiative through my online presence.

**Action:**  
I created and optimized my LinkedIn profile, including a professional photo, academic achievements, and project showcases.  
I followed industry leaders, joined ICT-related groups, and engaged in discussions to expand my professional reach.  
I also connected with classmates, lecturers, and professionals from the software development and applications field to broaden my network.

**Result:**  
I received positive engagement on my posts and connection requests from professionals in the tech community.  
This improved my understanding of how personal branding, consistency, and communication impact career opportunities.  
Through networking, I also learned about internship opportunities and professional certifications relevant to my career path.

**Skills Developed:**  
- Professional online communication  
- Personal branding and digital etiquette  
- Career-oriented networking  
- Online collaboration and engagement  

**Future Improvement:**  
I plan to grow my professional network further by attending virtual seminars, sharing project updates, and maintaining active engagement on LinkedIn.  
My next step is to build a small professional portfolio website linked directly from my LinkedIn page.
